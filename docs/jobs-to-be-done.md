# Jobs to be done

Each job describes what the person needs whether or not this app exists. Roles
are defined in [app-roles.md](app-roles.md).

## J-1: Know the full cost of a loan

**Role:** Prospective Borrower

When I am offered a loan that is presented by its monthly instalment, I want to
know the total amount I will pay back and how much of it is interest, so I can
judge the loan by its full cost rather than by the monthly figure alone.

## J-2: Know whether the repayment fits my budget

**Role:** Prospective Borrower

When I am deciding how much to borrow and over how many months, I want to know
the regular monthly amount I would be committing to, so I can check it fits my
budget before I sign.

## J-3: Weigh alternatives against each other

**Role:** Prospective Borrower

When I am weighing different amounts, rates or repayment periods, I want to know
how each change moves both the monthly amount and the total cost, so I can
choose knowingly between a lower monthly amount and a lower total cost.

## J-4: Understand how the debt goes down over time

**Role:** Prospective Borrower

When I am about to commit to years of repayments, I want to understand how each
payment divides between interest and reducing the debt, and how much I would
still owe at any point, so I can know what I would owe if my circumstances
changed part-way through.

## J-5: Know the interest still ahead of me

**Role:** Existing Borrower

When I am part-way through repaying a loan, I want to know how much interest I
will still pay on what I owe today, so I can decide whether to keep the loan as
it is, pay it down faster, or look for a cheaper one.

## J-6: Decide whether overpaying is worth it

**Role:** Existing Borrower

When I have money left over each month, I want to know how much sooner I would
be debt-free and how much interest I would avoid by paying more than the
required amount, so I can decide whether overpaying is a better use of that
money than keeping it.
